# Screen: Touch pads (part of Play)

## Behavior

- Big **L** and **R** hit targets (~44px+), bottom corners.
- One impulse per tap (no hold repeat).
- Keyboard still works (←/→, A/D).
- Center **FLING** between pads during station strips only.
